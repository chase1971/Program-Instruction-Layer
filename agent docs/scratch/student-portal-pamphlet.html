Math Apps Class

Student Portal

A personal web app for each student — one private link, no account or password. Practice, submit work, and see results while the instructor collects learning data safely.

Why it exists

The portal turns math apps and short assessments into a single place students actually use. Instead of guessing who struggled, you get item-level attempt data, exit-ticket responses, and sign-in activity — tied to real students on your machine, not exposed in the cloud.

What students get

Interactive practice apps

Guided tutorials and drills (e.g. Gauss–Jordan matrix method) with step-by-step progress saved automatically.

Quizzes & exit tickets

End-of-class check-ins and custom surveys — students answer on their phone or laptop in minutes.

Grades & announcements

Students see their scores and read class announcements without opening email or D2L.

Pause and resume

Work is saved mid-activity. A student can close the tab and pick up where they left off.

Privacy by design

The cloud database stores random codes and scores only — never student names or emails. The code-to-name map lives on the instructor's computer and Google Drive roster files. If the database were leaked, the data would not identify anyone.
