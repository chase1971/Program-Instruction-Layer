Teacher Console — five Macro App skins

Same layout as the shell you have now: sidebar nav, course pills, Dashboard cards (Recent exit tickets, Class snapshot, Announcements). What differs is color, shadow, and how “new” the console feels compared to the rest of Macro App.

A — Frost white cards

Pale gray workspace, white glossy cards, soft shadows. Blue only on active nav and selected course. Safest “modern dashboard” look.

Best if: you want it to feel clean without fighting Macro App’s existing tab bar.

B — Navy header band

Dark navy strip across the top of the console main area (like the student portal header), then white content below. Sidebar stays white with a navy left-border active indicator.

Best if: you want the student portal’s dark-blue / light-content pairing echoed on the teacher side.

C — Sidebar contrast

Light blue-gray sidebar vs pure white main canvas. Active nav is a solid blue button. Minimal shadows — very flat and airy.

Best if: you want the sidebar to read clearly separate from the content at a glance.

D — Glossy elevated panel

Whole console floats as one white rounded panel inside Macro App. Gradient blue active nav, strong card shadows — most “SaaS app” feel.

Best if: you want the biggest visual break from old Macro App styling.

E — Portal color match

Pale blue workspace tint, white cards with blue accent lines, pill nav matching the student portal mockup you liked. Closest family resemblance to what students see.

Best if: teacher and student surfaces should feel like the same product.

Implementation note

These apply only when activeModuleId === 'student-progress' — scoped CSS on .teacher-console-* classes, no change to Gradebook or other tabs. Pick a letter (or mix: e.g. E sidebar + A cards) and that becomes the CSS pass on the shell you already have.
