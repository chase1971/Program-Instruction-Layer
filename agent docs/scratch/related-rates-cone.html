Related rates — conical tank problem

Water pours into a downward-pointing cone at a constant rate. The surface radius is proportional to the depth (similar triangles, r/h = R/H) — a blue arrow shows that radius directly as it grows. Because the cross-section widens as the water rises, the depth's rise rate does the opposite of the square and ladder clips: flashes start fast near the point and land farther apart as the water climbs. Marks h = 4 cm, the depth the textbook question asks about. Sped up 1.5× from the real 10 cm³/sec pour-in rate.
