Related rates — ladder problem

A 13 ft ladder's foot is pulled away from the wall at a constant 2 ft/sec. The height on the wall flashes gold on every whole foot — the flashes land closer together as the ladder falls, because the top's rate of descent keeps climbing even though the foot's rate never does. Marks x = 5 ft, the distance the textbook question asks about. Plays in real time.
