What this episode is

He finally unpauses. Year one is economy babysitting (spinners, forestry, masonry shortage), locking Castile as an ally, then using the first parliament to fabricate on Aragon and declare with Castile called in. War starts at the end of the episode; the fighting is left for Ep 3.

Between episodesSwaps the free-subjects cabinet push for appease nobles. Nobles were too salty from privilege changes; he wants taxable, calm estates first.
Diplomatic winOnce Castile rivals France, he rivals France too. Alliance becomes available and he locks it immediately.
War goalCB on Aragon aimed at Barcelona / Catalonia for vibe, with room to take more or flip Mallorca as a subject if AE allows.
Situation: Across the Strait / Battle for the Strait fires when Castile pressures Granada. He watches it but does not redirect the Portugal plan around it.
