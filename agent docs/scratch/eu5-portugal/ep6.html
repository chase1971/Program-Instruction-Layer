What this episode is

Finishes the Castile–Morocco war on the sidelines, banks the plague recovery, then pivots hard into Age-2 exploration (Azores / nearby seas) while locking communalism and ripping noble privileges. Ends in a new parliament with noble rebels climbing and Aragon still the next CB target.

War endStays out of the last Morocco fighting. Castile takes land, reps, and money. Later accepts a Castile call to submit Granada and barely lifts a finger. Portuguese army maintenance drops once Castile is engaged.
EconomyUses the expand-forest discount for more Torres Novas lumber. Development continues (Minho / high-control targets). First Farah bailiff finally buys. Two exploration fleets launch from Lisboa despite no true explorers yet.
SocietyCabinet flips to communalism. Clergy are so happy he starts yoinking privileges. Nobles get salty fast; he appeases and throws Cortes support at them. Possible small noble revolt accepted as the cost.
Black Death already left Portuguese land. Roughly a quarter of the population died (~1.2M to ~900k). He calls that a strong result versus countries that lose a third or half.
