What this episode is

Peace into the Black Death. He leans hard into the peasant-powered identity (commoner mining, free subjects, food and lumber), procures remedies early, parks the army out of plague provinces, then accepts another Castile call into Morocco while still recovering. Ends mid-parliament eyeing Aragon again and communalism so he can revoke traditionalist privileges.

PlagueHits earlier than the ~1447 guess. Lands in Lisboa first (hospital pays off). Procure remedies for the run of the disaster. Avoid expensive forgiveness / food-export bans. Do not start offensive wars until levies refill.
BuildsMore lumber and food RGOs. Fishing villages on the coast for harbor capacity, maritime proximity, and trade. Tar kilns for the construction crunch. Skip Santarém hospital (too few pops).
