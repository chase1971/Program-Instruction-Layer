What this run is

Lookas et bella (About 30 min). Portugal on 1.4 / Across the Pillars. No European conquest. Rebuild the economy, survive Morocco, find the India route, then colonize and trade until 1517 with over 1,000 gold net from trade.

ThesisPortugal is a weak army and a strong trader. Castile and Morocco are the early threats. Skip the Strait fight unless an event saves Castile relations.
Four steps1) Rebuild economy. 2) Survive Moroccan invasions. 3) Discover the India expedition. 4) Colonize the modern way (trade posts, cabinet migration, selective coastal colonies).
End state (1517)Presence from Iberia along Africa to India, Indochina, China, Japan. Caribbean and South America started. Portugal itself not depopulated.
DLC decision that changes the run: Estadia (strong bonuses to moving goods and maintaining colonies). Later government forms: Casada India vs Estad India (mutually exclusive; he prefers researching into Casada India).

Opening: rebuild the economy

Day-one cuts and taxes

  • Disband army, delete surplus forts, set diplo spending to zero
  • Tariffs on imports of goods he can produce himself
  • With 50 influence: grant textile privileges in southern Portugal; add more privileges as influence regenerates
  • Research: cheaper food and faster institution spread
  • Improve relations / aim for alliances with Castile (Spain) and Aragon

After the first monthly tick

  • Build both textile guilds in the capital; develop wool RGOs that feed them
  • Only develop RGOs where control is already decent; prioritize food
  • Invest in religious orders (manpower even from the starting order)
  • Burgher privileges next: development, then taxation capacity
  • Then communalism privileges, then navy, then free subjects (first three values he pushes)
  • Cabinet: one advisor on those values, one cycling increase control (control growth much faster than decay)
Castile allianceSecured after about two years when attitude flipped. Then farm favors: invite Castilian settlers, occasionally ask for money. Drop Aragon as a needed ally once Castile is locked.
First parliamentMove itinerant court off Lisbon (bonus does not stack on capital) to Porto. Later: inland city once he can afford ~2,000 for a new city (Beja in his timeline).
Later parliamentsLegal reforms (tax burghers/peasants), maritime law toward maritime values, education laws. Renaissance event: diplomatic path for colonial subject loyalty.

Survive Morocco (and Granada)

Morocco is “an absolute monster” in this DLC. Castile often fields almost nothing. Portugal ends up defending Castile more than the reverse.

  1. Do not rush. Let Morocco push deep into Castile and split onto multiple fort sieges.
  2. Starve them. Wait until armies hit Portuguese forts and run out of supply (supply map + army tooltips). Light cavalry burns food fast.
  3. Only fight decisive wins. Keep the army selected; refuse battles the UI does not call a clear win.
  4. Strike when starved. Catch retreating or empty-supply stacks and wipe them.
  5. Cut the foothold. Capture Morocco’s Iberian supply base once their stacks are weakened.
  6. Peace terms. Transfer Moroccan land to Castile; take money for yourself. Do not keep North African conquests early.
  7. Repeat. Same tactic every 5 to 10 years when Morocco hits him or Castile. Prefer huge cash peaces to stretch truces.
Granada also piled on while Castile was empty. He crushed Granada’s stacks (low cavalry) but Castile still lost that war and likely the Strait race. He still refused to personally conquer Castile.
Black DeathSegregate infected (communalism). Buy indulgences near the end for stability.
Post-war moneyMoroccan merchant event dumped cash. Then roads along rivers, bridges, employment methods that favor infrastructure. ~12 years to ~40 gold income.
Alliance stackLater: England + Castile + Aragon just to reduce Morocco spam. Castile still needed babysitting.

Growth, exploration, Africa

Build and explore order

  • Naval explorations one at a time at first (expensive). Leaders: highest military skill (speed), then admin (cost).
  • Steal African maps from Morocco via espionage (repeat until Mali is revealed), then steal maps from Mali.
  • ~60 gold income: start urbanization. Coastal towns for port throughput + sailors. Cap about 3 towns per province (1–2 inland if needed).
  • Advisors push coastal development. Treasury bonds: half RGOs, half capital/Porto production, then bailiffs on rich RGOs.
  • Farming villages on food-producing RGOs (especially wool) for big RGO output. Weaken local nobility there; market access less important for RGO efficiency.
  • Colonize Canaries when found. Embrace institutions; rush colonial range + Portugal’s range techs.
  • Improve relations with Mali; build trade offices/outposts in range and extract gold as #2 trader there before conquering anything.

Africa wars

  • Into the Unknown event (~1405): take exploration speed bonuses immediately (stability be damned).
  • Round Cape by 1416. Ally Mali when too strong to conquer; use them, then hit neighbors (Benin with a small crusader/combined-arms, low-food professional stack).
  • Need malaria resistance. Numerical losses vs Benin were high at first; reset and return until levies are wiped.
  • Age of Discovery: take the diplomatic path for colonization speed and trade. Research trading posts, colonial range, migration, maritime trade efficiency.
  • First colonial nation (Benin area) → great power. Tax reform event (+5% max tax / efficiency) saved the budget.
  • Later: Jolof, weakened Mali, Zimbabwe gold/iron, Madagascar, more central Africa. African colonies rebel until yellow “integrated” territories calm down.

India route and Asian trade

New expedition systemAge of Discovery expeditions to India / New World, not only normal exploration. Dedicated explorers. Long route with historical trading-post and diplomacy events.
Do not fail the tripKeep expedition supplies and crew morale above zero or it aborts back to Lisbon with partial discovery only.
Unlock tipTo race India as Portugal, research the trading-post techs first. New World discovery wants its own institution/tech ASAP; Columbus birth event also accelerates it.
  • He found the India expedition ~1450 after rounding Africa (feels ~13 years “late” but still fine).
  • Events grant trading posts, faster colonies, and even a CB on Kilwa. Taking spices by cutting food ended his expedition early, but the route still counted as discovered.
  • Rewards: strong 30-year colonization/trade bonuses; sell spices; enact Thalassocracy ~20 years to push naval values and trade income toward ~100 gold.
  • Conquer Kilwa on the CB; release a subject (labor for later colonies).
  • India: only one weak coastal kingdom was takeable. Land the same crusader army. Improve relations with the huge neighbors (120k+ levies). Enact Casada India when researched (subject loyalty + trade); avoid rushing Estad India if you want Casada later.
  • Indochina next for cloves (“clothes” in the auto-transcript). Trade posts everywhere. China sold him locations (~70k colonists for 2,000). Stay polite; he cannot fight a Ming-scale army.

Colonization machine (Americas)

  1. Wait for Columbus if you want his exploration bonuses (he waited ~6 years after England discovered America).
  2. Fund a max supply / crew expedition. Discover South America coast → big research + 30-year exploration bonuses.
  3. Research indigenous policy, colonial policy, and cabinet abilities (critical as a low-pop country).
  4. Colonize high port-capacity spots and unique resources first. Do not settle next to Morocco early (they eat colonies).
  5. Indigenous policy: he forced labor / work because tribal→peasant conversion felt too slow (open to other takes).
  6. Cabinet migration: pick source pops from directly owned African land (not subjects), target an active colony. Colonists jump (e.g. 30 → 167). Colonial policy roughly doubled that.
  7. Mythic expeditions via Columbus: Cities of Gold / El Dorado (huge local gold), Fountain of Youth (Florida), lost kingdoms (research). Circumnavigation succeeded even though Columbus died mid-voyage.
  8. World division (~1502) with England: propose Lisbon. Before it locks, spam colonists onto low-base-pop provinces and dump African pops via cabinet for 3%+/month completion so you keep the claim.
  9. To spare Portuguese population: only run as many colonies as you have advisors supporting them; source migrants from Africa / conquered pops that already sit on colonial land.
Long-term if continuing past 1517: finish South America for potatoes and corn. Factory-of-Europe plan: shift traditional to capital economy over 30 to 40 years and pull African/Asian goods home through the trade-post network.
