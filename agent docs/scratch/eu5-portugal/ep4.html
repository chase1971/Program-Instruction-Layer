What this episode is

Castile gets jumped by Morocco (and friends). He accepts the call, attaches to Castile for a short bloody war with no Portuguese land, then spends the peace building hospitals and locking in a peasant-powered economy: free subjects, balance-the-budget inflation burn, and commoner mining law through parliament.

War shapeDefensive call from Castile. Raise levies, merge into one stack, attach to Castile. No navy push. Peace is annul Morocco–Tunis, money/reps; Portugal takes no land. Granada ends up independent of Castile, which he marks as a future opportunity.
Plague prepHospital cash finally appears after the short war. Porto first (highest pop), then Lisboa. About three years left on the Black Death clock by the end.
Society betCommoner mining operations via parliament. More peasant happiness and free-subjects push so he can tax commoners harder. Nobles get salty; cabinet flips to appease them.
He still wants another balance-the-budget later to walk inflation the rest of the way to zero. Minting was an early boost; he does not want to keep doing inflation tricks.
