Related rates — square problem

A square's side grows at a constant rate. The area value ticks up and flashes gold every time it crosses a whole number — the flashes land closer together as the clip goes on, because the area's rate keeps climbing even though the side's rate never does. Sped up from the textbook's 3 in/min so the pattern is visible in a few seconds; the side still runs up to 9 in.
