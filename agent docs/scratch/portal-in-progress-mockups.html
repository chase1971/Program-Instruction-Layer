In-progress app tile — 5 concepts

Tap the tile to continue; each concept shows restart/abandon differently. Pick a direction for implementation.

1 — Badge on icon + subtitle

Amber dot on icon; “Question 2 of 3” under title. Single tap target; restart elsewhere.

  • Minimal change
  • Abandon not on tile

2 — Progress ring on icon

Ring shows 2/3; pill + progress bar under title.

  • Progress obvious at a glance
  • Busier layout

3 — Split tile (Continue | Start over)

Two tap zones on one card — needs confirm before abandon.

  • Both actions on tile
  • Risk of mis-tap on restart

4 — Taller tile with two buttons

Continue + Abandon as equal buttons inside the card.

  • Best for dwell-click
  • Taller tile

5 — Icon overlay + restart below

Pause/2/3 on icon; separate “Start new attempt” pill below tile.

  • Status on icon
  • Restart separated from continue
